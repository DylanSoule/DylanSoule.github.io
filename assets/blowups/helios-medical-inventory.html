NASA Hunch Internship

HELIOS - Medical Inventory System

A concept medical inventory workflow designed for a lunar operations environment, where staff need fast visibility into supply levels, expiration risk, and restock urgency.

Role

Student intern and contributor to planning, interface concepting, and system flow design.

Focus Areas

  • Inventory status by category, location, and urgency
  • Simple restock indicators and expiration-aware alerts
  • Workflow clarity for low-bandwidth and high-stress conditions

What I Learned

This project reinforced the value of building interfaces around mission constraints first, then layering on detail only where it improves reliability and speed of decision-making.
